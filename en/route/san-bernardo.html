Hot Springs and Waterfall

Hike 466 metres up to a waterfall, then finish soaking in warm water.

The town's long outing, heading south into San Bernardo. You pass the old saman tree by the bridge, climb to the El Chorrerón waterfall — a white thread falling through the gorge between two mountains — and end the day at Aguascalientes, a warm pool among huge boulders, with the Soma grill beside it for food.

  • DurationFull day · nearly two hours each way
  • DifficultyModerate · the last stretch is rough track, kept damp by the spray
  • Starts atTown centre, heading toward San Bernardo
  • WhenLeave early: it is nearly two hours out, counting the drive. There is no phone signal on the way.
  • HowCan be done alone, better with a guide
What you take away

One road gives you both faces of water in a single day: the kind that falls hard from 466 metres above, and the kind that comes out of the ground warm enough to sit in.

Tip: Bring cash: there is a crossing fee at the Soma entrance. Grippy shoes and a downloaded map before you leave, since there is no signal. The El Chorrerón and Aguascalientes tracks were GPS-recorded and are drawn on the site map.

The stops, in order

  1. If you have time

    The Samán Tree

    ~2.7 km from town · Easy access · Open during the day

    It sits right on the road, 2.7 km from town, so it is not a detour: it is a five-minute stop. A huge old saman tree at the edge of the bridge, its crown spread like an umbrella over the road. Bromeliads, orchids, ferns and old-man's-beard live in its branches: a hanging garden above the roadway.

    See the full entry · Get directions

  2. Main stop

    El Chorrerón Waterfall

    ~8 km from the Zambrano bridge · +466 m · Moderate · ~35 min by vehicle + ~30 min by motorbike and on foot

    Eight kilometres from the Zambrano bridge and 466 metres of climb. From afar it looks like a white thread on the green; up close it falls down a rock wall throwing up permanent spray. The arrival is marked by a gateway of two posts and a wooden crossbeam with the name carved into it, the waterfall right behind. Do this first, on fresh legs.

    See the full entry · Get directions

  3. Main stop

    Soma Grill & Hot Springs

    15.5 km from Labateca · ~1 km on foot to the pool · Easy · the path to the pool is short and has a handrail · Saturdays and Sundays, 8:00 a.m. – 9:00 p.m.

    The end of the day, and it is meant that way: the El Chorrerón entry itself recommends it. From the gate to the pool is about 740 metres along a dirt path with a bamboo handrail. The pool is warm water among huge boulders polished by the river. Up at the grill there are meats on the fire, bamboo shelters, a fire pit and a lookout with beanbags. Run by Sofía Parada.
