Páramo Route

More than 2,000 hectares of Labateca lie within the Santurbán Páramo.

Labateca holds over 2,000 hectares inside the Santurbán Páramo, including Laguna Negra and the high sectors toward La Ovejera. We have not yet surveyed these sites in the field: we have no verified coordinates, no trailhead and no measured times. Rather than publish an invented route, we would rather say so: this one is in preparation. If you want to go up, talk to the local guide and he will tell you what is possible today.
