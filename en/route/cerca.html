Waterfall and Lookout, Half a Day

A waterfall and lunch with a view, all within three kilometres.

The easiest outing in town to organise: climb to the Siscata Waterfall on a trail that starts in the town itself, then finish with lunch at El Pedregal, a lookout farm with views across to the neighbouring town of Toledo. There and back in one long morning.

  • Duration3–4 hours
  • DifficultyModerate · 3.6 km on foot and +342 m of climb to the waterfall
  • Starts atTown centre
  • WhenEl Pedregal serves on weekends, so the full route is a Saturday or Sunday plan. Try to be at the waterfall around 10 a.m., when the sun reaches it.
  • HowSelf-guided, using the site map
What you take away

You need neither a whole day nor a car to see falling water in Labateca: the first waterfall starts where the last street ends.

Tip: Grippy shoes and a downloaded map before you leave: the Siscata track was GPS-recorded and can be followed without signal. Climb first, eat afterwards.

The stops, in order

  1. Main stop

    Siscata Waterfall

    ~3.6 km on foot · +342 m · Moderate · ~1 h 15 min on foot (or car/bike + walk)

    The trail starts in the town centre and climbs 342 metres over some 3.6 km. The track was GPS-recorded in the field, so you can follow it on the map even with no signal. Arrive around ten in the morning: that is when the sun enters the gorge and the fall is fully lit.

    See the full entry · Get directions

  2. Main stop

    El Pedregal Viewpoint

    ~1.5 km from town · Easy access · Weekends

    A farm a kilometre and a half from town that combines three things rarely found together: a lookout, a kitchen, and a Holy Sepulchre with crosses on the same grounds. From the terrace you see the mountains and, beyond, Toledo. Run by don Óscar Rozo.
