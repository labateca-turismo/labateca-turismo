Marian Route

An entire town organised around an apparition.

Five feast days a year, a monthly pilgrimage, seven carved stations on the climb and a chapel at the edge of town to bless those leaving. Devotion to the Virgin is not decoration in Labateca: it sets the calendar, the road and the books where family history is written.

  • Duration2–3 hours, on foot
  • DifficultyEasy · the Marian Trail is short but entirely uphill
  • Starts atOur Lady of Sorrows Parish
  • WhenAny day. There is a pilgrimage along the Marian Trail on the first Saturday of each month. The patron saint's five solemnities fall on 2 and 15 February, 15 August, 21 November and 8 December; the town fills up on those days.
  • HowSelf-guided, using the site map
What you take away

The patron saint did not appear in the town but in Bochagá, and still the town arranged itself around her: her five feasts rule the year, and in the churchyard the burial records reach back to 1712.

Tip: Start early and enter the church before or after the 7 a.m. mass so as not to interrupt. The Marian Trail is best climbed in the morning, in the shade. Visit the churchyard respectfully.

The stops, in order

  1. Main stop

    Our Lady of Sorrows Parish

    In town center · Easy access · Mass: Mon–Sat 7 am & 6 pm · Sun 7 & 10 am, 3 & 6 pm

    Begin where she is. The patron's image is venerated in the niche above the main altar, on a carved wooden throne, surrounded by hanging gold «milagros»: small figures devotees offer when a favour is granted. Each one is a thank-you with a name and a date, even if neither is written on it.

    See the full entry · Get directions

  2. Main stop

    Marian Trail

    ~900 m uphill from the edge of town · Easy–moderate · short but all uphill · Always open · pilgrimage on the first Saturday of every month

    Nine hundred metres uphill with seven stations for the Seven Sorrows of Mary: Simeon's prophecy, the flight into Egypt, the child lost in the temple, and on to the burial. Each station is a carved relief with its plaque, its scripture and its prayer. Climb slowly: it was made to be read, not covered.

    See the full entry · Get directions

  3. Stop

    Labateca Cemetery

    In town center · Easy access · Open during the day

    The churchyard has been tied to the parish from the start. The parish keeps burial records dating to 1712: three centuries of Labateca surnames in order. It is the town's oldest and most consulted archive, and the reason an entire family can be reconstructed here.

    See the full entry · Get directions

  4. If you have time

    Shrine of Our Lady of Mount Carmel

    500 m from the main square · Easy access · Open all day

    The last stop before leaving town, beside the bullring. In Colombia the Virgin of Carmen is the patron of drivers, which is why her chapel sits right at the roadside: those leaving cross themselves as they pass and commend the journey to her. On 16 July cars and motorbikes are blessed here.

    See the full entry · Get directions
