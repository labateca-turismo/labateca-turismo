Where to stay in Labateca

Labateca is a small town and formal accommodation is still scarce: this guide currently lists a single place to stay, and we say so plainly so nobody arrives assuming otherwise. There is at least one more in town that is not listed here — we only publish what the owner has authorized — so it is worth asking once you arrive. If you are coming from far away, message ahead on WhatsApp. Many visitors also base themselves in Pamplona or Cúcuta and come up for the day.
