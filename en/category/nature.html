Waterfalls and nature in Labateca

What draws visitors from outside: the Lirgua and Dos Brazos waterfalls, the Siscatá falls — reached on foot along a trail, not by car —, the La Peña recreation spot and the centuries-old saman tree. Bring shoes with grip and water; in the rainy season the paths get slippery and the rivers run high.

    Mountain waterfall reached by a trail from Labateca town center. The route was field-recorded with GPS.

    ~1 h 15 min on foot (or car/bike + walk)
