Where to eat in Labateca

The day in Labateca starts at the bakery and at the cafés that open at 6:30 a.m. with black coffee, empanadas and broth. Lunch is the set menu; by the afternoon come the burgers, the ice cream and the grill, and on weekends the bars and open-air spots beside the football pitch. Almost everything is a few blocks from the main square, so you can walk from one to the next.
