What to see in Labateca

Labateca was founded as a pueblo de indios on 19 July 1623, and what remains of that history can be seen on foot: the parish church of Nuestra Señora de las Angustias with its patron saint, the main square, the cemetery and, outside town, the Tomb of Cacique Bateca, where people still leave offerings. The whole town centre can be walked in a morning.
