A town is not
only its places

It is also the dates when it fills up, and the people who fill it. Here are the fairs and festivals of the year —when they fall and what happens at each one— and the people of Labateca whose work is known well beyond these mountains.

The year

When the town dresses up

Five dates mark the municipal calendar. Two of them move the whole town: the February fair and the patron saint's feast in November, which are separate celebrations and have always been kept that way.

    The exact dates for each year are published by the Mayor's Office of Labateca. Holy Week moves with the liturgical calendar. If you are travelling for one particular festival, it is worth confirming the day first.

    Films are made here

    That is not a figure of speech. Short films shot in these streets, in these pastures and in this church have gone on to screen in Cine Colombia theatres and at festivals in Chile, Brazil and Indonesia.

    David Andrés Flechas Leal

    Film producer, screenwriter and director · born in Labateca

    He holds a degree in Film and Television Directing and Production from Universidad Manuela Beltrán, and a master's in Creative Audiovisual Production from Universidad del Magdalena. He completed his training with diplomas and workshops in creative directing, screenwriting and assistant directing at the Escuela Nacional de Cine (ENACC) and at Congo Films School.

    He has produced, written and directed more than twenty short films. Several have been selected for international festivals —FICValdivia in Chile, Minikino Film Week in Indonesia, the ComKids Festival in Brazil, Bogoshorts and Calibélula— and others have screened in commercial theatres at Cine Colombia, Cinépolis, Cinemark and Cineland.

    What brings him to this page is not only his CV: it is that he has brought cinema to Labateca. Under his lead, several audiovisual projects have been made here, from the territory and with people from the municipality taking part. Among them Al otro lado de la montaña, La Esperanza, Promesa and Pensándote.

    The short film

    Disneylandia

    2021 · Fiction · 8 minutes · Colombia

    The video does not load until you tap it: until then, this page asks YouTube for nothing.

    Watch the full short film on RTVCPlay

    José, an eight-year-old farm boy, rides his bicycle to school along a country track in Norte de Santander. The day he realises it is Mother's Day, an impossible present lodges itself in his head: taking his mum to Disneyland. To raise the money he will have to decide whether to give up the one thing that is truly his.

    And many people are still missing

    This section is only beginning. Labateca has musicians, teachers, craftspeople, athletes and people who went far away and still name the town. We will add them one by one, and each one goes in only if they —or their family— say yes. That is how the guide's places were built, and that is how this will be built.

    Do you know someone who should be on this page? It could be a relative, a neighbour or yourself. Write to us with the name and the reason, and we will take care of the rest.
