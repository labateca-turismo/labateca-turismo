Privacy and data protection policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026 · Law 1581 of 2012 (Colombia)

This is a translation for convenience. The binding text is the Spanish version, which prevails in case of any discrepancy. Write to labatecacolombia@gmail.com for any request.

1. Data controller

The party responsible for the personal data collected on this site is Labateca Turismo, a tourism initiative of the municipality of Labateca, Norte de Santander, Colombia.

  • Email: labatecacolombia@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: +57 320 906 0582

2. What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and the message you send us, all given voluntarily.
  • Visitor reviews: the name you choose to display, your opinion, the rating and, if you attach one, a photo of your visit.
  • Preferences on your device: favourites, "My route" and language are stored only in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device and we never see them.
  • Location: only if you press "Calculate distance". It is used there and then to work out the distance and is not stored.

3. Purpose

We use this data to answer your enquiries, improve the guide, and display reviews (after moderation). We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

4. Third-party services

To work, the site relies on providers that may process technical data (such as your IP address) under their own policies:

  • Cloudflare (hosting and security).
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (visit statistics). It tells us how many people come in and which pages are read, with no cookies, no browser fingerprinting and without identifying anyone. It is not shared with advertisers.
  • Web3Forms (delivers the contact form by email).
  • Cloudinary (image storage).
  • Open-Meteo / MET Norway (live weather).
  • Google Maps (opens only when you press "How to get there").
  • OpenStreetMap (maps).

5. Cookies

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies. It only uses local storage (localStorage) in your browser to remember your preferences, which you can clear at any time from your browser settings.

6. Your rights as a data subject

Under Law 1581 of 2012 you have the right to know, update, correct and delete your data, and to withdraw your authorisation. To exercise these rights, write to labatecacolombia@gmail.com and we will handle your request.

7. Visitor reviews and photos

By submitting a review or a photo, you declare that the content is yours or that you are authorised to publish it, and you allow us to display it on the site. If a photo includes identifiable people, you must have their consent. You can ask us to remove your review at any time.

8. Collaborators and tourism partners (businesses, lodgings and guides)

To promote tourism in the municipality, this site publishes information about local collaborators (shops, restaurants, hotels, accompanying guides and other partners): the name of the business or person, contact details (phone, WhatsApp, address), a description of the service and photographs of the premises or of the person.

  • Prior, express and informed authorisation. This information is published only with each collaborator's consent, collected through the project's authorisation form, under Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013.
  • Purpose. To make the collaborator's establishment or service known and to promote tourism in Labateca on this website, the app and our social media.
  • Image rights. Where photographs of people are published, we hold their authorisation to use their image for that purpose. This consent is revocable at any time.
  • Voluntary and revocable. Providing this data is voluntary. A collaborator may at any time ask for their information to be updated, corrected or deleted, or withdraw their authorisation, by writing to labatecacolombia@gmail.com.

We keep proof of each authorisation for as long as the information remains published. We do not register databases with the RNBD of Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC), as this initiative does not reach the thresholds that make such registration mandatory; should that change, we will update this policy.

9. Security and minors

We apply reasonable measures to protect the data. The site is aimed at a general audience; we do not knowingly collect data from minors without the authorisation of their parents or guardians. Photographs of collaborators must not include minors or identifiable third parties without their consent.

10. Term and changes

This policy may be updated. We will publish any change on this same page, with its date.

Note: Are you a collaborator who would like to appear in the guide? Use the data and image authorisation form. That document is a base template; legal review is recommended before definitive use, particularly if the project moves to handling data commercially.
