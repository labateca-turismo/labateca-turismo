AM-BAR Café

Bar and café in the town center of Labateca, a few steps from the Main Square. It opens at night: you'll know the front by the blue neon sign above the door. Inside there are two areas — a room with wooden tables and benches, and a corner with sofas around a low table — yellow-and-burgundy tiled floors, string lights across the ceiling and a black wall with a neon sign. Owner: Leonardo Hernández. Open Friday, Saturday and Sunday, 3:00 p.m. to 12:00 a.m.
