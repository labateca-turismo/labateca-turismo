Galactic Bar

A space-themed bar in Labateca's town center, on the corner of the main square. A roomy, colorful spot to hang out with friends: beers, sodas, snacks, music and good vibes. Adults only. On weekends it's the town's go-to meeting point; on weekdays they take group reservations. Run by its owner, Yuleysy Mariño.
