EXCII · Boutique & Accessories

A clothing and accessories boutique in the town centre, run by Eddy Lisbeth Parada. It is a large room with a pale floor and white light, the stock laid out by section: a gold hoop rack in the middle of the floor with whatever has just come in, one whole wall of shirts and tees, another of jackets and hoodies, a unit of jeans folded by size, and a corner for underwear and socks. There is womenswear, menswear and children's. On the counter, under glass, are the accessories: caps, earrings, hair ties, costume jewellery and perfume. And there is a second business you would not guess from the street: EXCII also handles paperwork — printing, photocopying, comptroller, inspector-general and land-registry certificates, copies of deeds, rental and sale contracts, vehicle paperwork, transfer of ownership for Venezuelan-plated vehicles, and IGAC land-registry procedures.

Tip: It closes at midday and reopens at three, so the morning window is the short one if you are passing through. For a traveller the useful part is not only the clothes — if you need something printed, a photocopy, or an online certificate pulled, they do it here, and there are not many places in town that do. You can ask ahead on WhatsApp at +57 314 200 0824.
