La Esquinita del Sabor — Café & Snacks

A café and quick-bites spot in Labateca's town center, with a colorful interior decorated in Colombian-flag colors, flowers and potted plants. Great for breakfast and afternoon snacks: freshly made empanadas, pastries and arepas, bread, oat drinks and cold beverages. A cozy corner with wooden tables to sit and share. Run by Doña Yina Ochoa.
