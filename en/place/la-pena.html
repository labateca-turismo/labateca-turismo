La Peña Ecotourism Center

An ecotourism farm southwest of Labateca (Tencalá area). It offers hiking to the top of La Peña —with the option to go up one way and come down by the lagoon—, Andean condor and birdwatching, contact with local animals (goats and sheep), and a visit to Indigenous tombs and remains with local historical background. It has a camping area and meal service, with lamb (ovejo) as the specialty. Hosted by Mr. Víctor Antonio Daza.

Tip: Great for camping and spotting the Andean condor. They have 4 tents of their own; for large groups, bring your own gear.
