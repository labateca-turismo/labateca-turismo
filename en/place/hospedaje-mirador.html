Mirador de Mi Bateca Lodging

Lodging in Labateca set in an eye-catching building decorated with landscape murals and regional motifs. It offers private rooms and a shared dormitory, a bathroom, a lounge and beautiful panoramic views of the valley and mountains. The same venue has a disco-bar and karaoke area, 'Mirador de mi Bateca', ideal for groups and visitors. Available 24 hours by arrangement with the manager. Owner: Fredy Delgado.
