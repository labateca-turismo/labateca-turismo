Bike Rafa Roadside Stop

A roadside stop a little over a kilometer north of town, right on the highway beside the riverbed. The house is painted in the style of Colombia's coffee towns: yellow, pink and blue skirting, brightly colored doors and windows, leaves hanging from the eaves. Under the porch there's a display counter, round tables and chairs; behind it, a garden with tin sunflowers on the fence, a wooden swing and fruit trees. Out front by the road, painted wheel rims and tires serve as planters at the foot of a Colombian flag. Owner: Rafael Nilson Parada Vargas.
