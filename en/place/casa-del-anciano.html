Teresita de Lisieux Home for the Elderly

The Santa Teresita de Lisieux Centre for the Life and Wellbeing of Older Adults — the town's care home, which everyone calls 'Hogar de Vida' — opened on 21 November 2003. The plaque at the entrance records who made it possible: the administration of mayor José Eugenio Cáceres S. (2001-2003) with treasurer Tito A. Capacho R., the Municipal Council chaired by Evelio Valencia P., the Association of Teresian Alumni advised by Sister María E. Gómez V., and the people of Labateca at home and abroad. It closes with a line from Saint Thérèse: 'Nothing is small when love is great.' It is a single-storey house with wide red-tiled corridors, a garden and a terrace looking out over the whole valley. The photographs on this page are published by permission of the Municipality of Labateca (Resolution 322 of 25 August 2026).

Getting there
On the south-western edge of town, on the road leading out. It is the long single-storey building with 'Hogar de Vida Sta. Teresita Lisieux' painted above the window and a red iron gate.

Tip: If you visit, there are rules and they are meant seriously: sign the visitors' book and stay in the main hall, not the bedrooms, and do not leave cash with residents. Soft drinks, boxed or bottled juices, chocolates and sweets are not allowed either: they upset the residents' diets and health. What they do accept, and need, are donations of clothing, food, medicine and money. One nice detail: the public library comes up now and then to read aloud to them.
