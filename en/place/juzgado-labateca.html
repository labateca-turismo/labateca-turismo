Labateca Municipal Court (Juzgado Promiscuo Municipal)

The town's court, part of Colombia's Judicial Branch (Judicial District of Pamplona, Norte de Santander). Being a «promiscuo» (mixed-jurisdiction) court, a single judge hears cases across several areas of law: civil, criminal, family, labor, commercial and other first-instance and lower-amount matters. Here citizens file lawsuits and constitutional-rights actions (tutelas), attend hearings, pursue their cases, request copies and receive guidance on judicial procedures. Case dockets, transfer lists and procedural notices are consulted online at ramajudicial.gov.co, and the office can be reached at its official email jprmlabateca@cendoj.ramajudicial.gov.co.
