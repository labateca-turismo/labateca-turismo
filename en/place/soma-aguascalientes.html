Soma Grill & Hot Springs

An open-air riverside spot with a hot-water pool reached on foot from the entrance. It lies within San Bernardo, on the same road that climbs to the El Chorrerón waterfall, so it is usually done on the same outing. The place has bamboo-framed shelters with zinc roofs, tables with red umbrellas on the lawn, a fire pit, a lookout with beanbags open to the mountainside, and string lights for the evening. The kitchen turns out grilled meats and ribs, burgers and cocktails. The pool is about 740 meters in from the entrance, along a dirt path with a bamboo handrail: a warm pool among huge boulders polished smooth by the river. Owner: Sofía Parada.

Access
Easy · the path to the pool is short and has a handrail
Getting there
From Labateca it is about 39 minutes and 15.5 km by road toward San Bernardo — as marked on the signpost at the site itself. The entrance is on the road, to the right after the bridge, under a metal arch with the SOMA sign. From there it is a scant kilometer on foot to the pool, with the GPS route drawn on the map.
