Café BTK

Coffee from Labateca, roasted and packed on the same property as the hardware store, 500 meters from the main square. Today it does two things: it roasts coffee as a service, and it sells its own processed coffee — ground, in 250-gram bags and in boxes of 15 drip sachets. The packaging tells you what is inside: grown in Labateca, Norte de Santander, at 1,676 meters above sea level, Castillo variety, medium-high roast, an even and balanced cup with sweet, floral and chocolatey notes. The initials stand for Cacique Bateca, who appears on the round sign at the entrance. Managed by Javier Montañez.

Getting there
Turn in at the same roofed gateway as the hardware store, heading out of town to the northwest; the sign on the road reads Ferretería · Vivero El Tulipán. Once on the property, the shop is the window under the round yellow Café BTK sign.

Tip: So you do not arrive expecting the wrong thing: this is not a coffee shop, you cannot sit down for a cup. You go for the packed coffee, or to have your own harvest roasted. It is worth the trip up anyway: from the yard you look down on the whole town, church tower and mountains behind.
