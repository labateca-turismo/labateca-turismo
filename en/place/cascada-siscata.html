Siscata Waterfall

Mountain waterfall reached by a trail from Labateca town center. The route was field-recorded with GPS.

Get directions See the trail See it on the map
Hours
~1 h 15 min on foot (or car/bike + walk)
Where it is
~3.6 km on foot · +342 m
Access
Moderate
Getting there
There are three ways to get there: (1) On foot from town: ~3.6 km with +342 m of elevation gain (~1 h 15 min). (2) By car up to Finca El Pedregal —cars go that far— then on foot: ~20 min to Mr. Epifanio's house (the last house) and ~25 min more to the waterfall. (3) By motorbike up to Mr. Epifanio's house, then ~25 min on foot. Follow the GPS route drawn on the map.

Tip: Aim to reach the waterfall around 10 a.m. to enjoy the sun and the beautiful scenery. Wear grippy footwear and download the offline map before leaving.
