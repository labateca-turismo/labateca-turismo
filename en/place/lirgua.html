Lirgua and Dos Brazos Waterfalls

Two waterfalls on the same creek, inside private land about 6 km west of the town center. The first is Lirgua Falls, a tall, strong drop landing in a pool ringed by rock and forest. Further upstream is Dos Brazos Falls, which splits into two parallel jets over a moss-covered stone wall. The trail between them is maintained by the owner's family: stone steps, planted gardens and hand-painted wooden signs. The full route is 1.6 km, climbs about 140 metres (from 1,620 to 1,760 m above sea level) and includes one creek crossing. This is private property belonging to Luis Fidel Ortiz Parada: to get in you must ask for the keys at the house next to the Lirgua bridge, and there is a per-person entrance fee.

Getting there
About 6 km west-southwest of the town center. Drive or ride to the Lirgua bridge; the house right next to the bridge is where you ask for the gate keys. The trail starts at the entrance: Lirgua Falls first, then Dos Brazos Falls further up the creek.

Tip: Ask for the keys at the house beside the Lirgua bridge before heading up; there is a per-person entrance fee. Wear non-slip shoes: the trail has stone steps, muddy stretches and a creek crossing. Go early and in daylight. Remember it is private land cared for by a family: take your trash with you and stay on the paths.
