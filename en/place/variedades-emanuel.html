Variedades Emanuel

A household variety store in the lower part of town. You do not come here on a whim but for what needs replacing: mattresses, suitcases and bags, umbrellas, fabrics, blankets, stationery and school supplies, toys, balloons and party goods, and the usual snacks in the case by the door. A Welcome mat at the entrance and narrow aisles between shelves stacked to the top. Run by its owner, Jesús Cañas.
