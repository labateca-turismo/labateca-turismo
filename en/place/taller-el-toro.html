El Toro Motorcycle Workshop

A motorcycle parts store and repair workshop in Labateca's town center. They sell parts for the main brands (Yamaha, Honda, Suzuki, Bajaj, AKT, Kymco and more), oils and lubricants, tires, batteries, spark plugs and accessories. Services include general mechanics, electrical work, threading/fasteners and hydraulic-press service. A well-stocked, organized shop to keep your motorcycle running. Owner: Gerson Enrique Fernández.
