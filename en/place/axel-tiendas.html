Axel Tiendas — Clothing & Variety Store

Clothing and variety store in Labateca's town center — 'to look and feel good.' You'll find clothing for the whole family (including children's wear), backpacks and plush toys, stationery and supplies, costume jewelry and accessories, snacks, candy and personal-care items. Run by Ana Karina Murillo.
