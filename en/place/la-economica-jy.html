La Económica JY — Minimarket

Well-stocked store and minimarket in Labateca's town center. You'll find groceries and grains, household cleaning and personal-care products, snacks and candy, bread, dairy, cold drinks, beer and sodas, water and convenience items. Run by Jheni Contreras.
