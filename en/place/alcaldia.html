Labateca Town Hall

The Municipal Palace is Labateca's seat of government and, inside, one of the handsomest spaces in town: two floors around a clay-tiled courtyard with a carved rosette in the floor, iron railings and two large painted murals — one of the harvest, with coffee, plantain and local fruit; the other of the old town, with the church and its tiled roofs. A bronze plaque by the entrance dates the building: it was completed on 7 August 1961, under governor Miguel García Herreros, with Antonio Guerrero Gallardo as comptroller, Germán Hernández Duplat at Public Works and architect Jaime Pacheco S. It houses the Mayor's Office, the Secretariats of Government, Planning, Finance, Social Development, Agriculture and Tourism, Internal Control, the Sisbén desk, the Family Commissariat, the Police Inspectorate, the Ombudsman, the Civil Registry and the Municipal Court. The photographs on this page are published by permission of the Municipality of Labateca (Resolution 322 of 25 August 2026).
