Hotel Los Robles

A hotel in a two-story colonial house facing the Main Square, with a central courtyard, a wooden-balustrade gallery running around the upper floor and colorful tiled floors. It is set up as a small museum of the town: oil portraits of Labateca's local characters hang along the walls, and beneath each one sits an object from another era — valve radios, a Motorola record player, a typewriter with a stack of vinyl LPs, an iron scale, a travel suitcase, a tube television. The rooms are simple, with a bed, bedside table and bathroom. Open 24 hours. Owner: Óscar Orlando Rozo Parada.
